## 3. Assessment tool — per-dimension introductory text (items 14-19)

These have no old text ("Geen tekst") — they are new intro paragraphs to add under each
dimension's heading at the top of that dimension's page in the assessment.

**14 — Governance**
> This section assesses how your data space is organized and managed. The questions
> focus on governance structures, decision-making, the rulebook, operational processes,
> participant management, and support mechanisms. Together, these elements demonstrate
> how effectively the data space can operate, onboard participants, manage change, and
> ensure long-term sustainability.

**15 — Business**
> This section evaluates the business foundations of your data space. The questions
> examine business goals, funding and revenue models, value propositions, use cases,
> participant engagement, data sharing activities, and the creation of value-added
> services. The objective is to understand whether the data space can deliver
> sustainable value to its participants over time.

**16 — Legal**
> This section assesses the legal readiness of your data space. The questions focus on
> identifying applicable regulations, translating them into requirements, ensuring
> compliance, and establishing agreements that govern participation, data sharing, and
> service provision. Together, these elements help create a legally sound environment
> for trusted data exchange.

**17 — Interoperability**
> This section evaluates how effectively participants can exchange and understand data.
> The questions cover data models, semantic standards, reference datasets, APIs, data
> exchange protocols, and mechanisms for provenance, observability, and traceability.
> These capabilities enable consistent, reliable, and scalable data sharing both within
> and across data spaces.

**18 — Control over Data & Trust**
> This section assesses the mechanisms that enable participants to share data with
> confidence while retaining control. The questions focus on digital identity, verifiable
> credentials, trust frameworks, compliance verification, registries, and the enforcement
> of access, usage, and consent policies. Together, these capabilities establish trust,
> security, and data sovereignty within the data space.

**19 — Value Creation**
> This section evaluates how your data space enables participants to discover, use, and
> create value from shared data. The questions focus on data and service descriptions,
> catalogues, value creation services, federation with other data spaces, participant
> adoption, data reuse, satisfaction, and the generation of new services and offerings.
> The goal is to understand the tangible value produced by the data space ecosystem.

## 4. Voorblad questionnaire (assessment welcome/intro screen)

Shown once, before any question appears.

### Welcome

Welcome to the **Data Space Maturity Assessment (DSMA)**. This self-assessment helps you
evaluate the maturity of your data space initiative using the CEN/CLC/TS 18331:2026 Data
Space Maturity Assessment standard.

*(Note: the source PDF's raw text repeats "Welcome to the Data Space Maturty Assessment
(DSMA)." once with a typo ("Maturty") before the clean sentence above — treat the bolded
clean sentence as the intended final copy, not a duplicate paragraph.)*

### What you will assess

The assessment covers six dimensions that target different aspects of maturity. These are:

- Governance
- Business
- Legal
- Interoperability
- Control over Data & Trust
- Value Creation.

Together, these dimensions provide a comprehensive view of your data space's current
level of maturity.

### What you will receive

Upon completion you will receive a **dashboard with the current level of maturity** and
the ability to download a separate **report.** This report will be used during value
coaching by the Data Space support center.

### Before you start

- Answer the questions based on your **current state** of the data space.
- Select the option that best reflects what is **implemented in practice**.
- If multiple people are involved in your initiative, consider completing the assessment
  collaboratively for the most accurate result.

### Time required

The assessment contains **52 questions** and typically takes **15-20 minutes** to
complete. There are no right or wrong answers. The goal is to establish a realistic
baseline that helps your data space prioritize its next steps in development and growth.
