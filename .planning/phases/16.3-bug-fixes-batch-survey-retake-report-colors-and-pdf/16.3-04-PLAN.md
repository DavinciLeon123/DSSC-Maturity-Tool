**Fix #5a (clipped dot):** flatten the nested flex in the "Submitted answers" row so the dot and score are direct children of `.answers-row` itself (removing the inner wrapper `div` entirely), matching the `.priority-row` pattern's use of `flex-shrink: 0` and a fixed-width score column:
```html
<div class="answers-row" style="display: flex; align-items: flex-start; gap: 16px; padding: 12px 0; border-bottom: 1px solid #f0f0f0;">
  <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 0;">
    <div style="font-size: 14px; color: #1a1a2e; margin-bottom: 4px;">{{ answer.text }}</div>
    <div style="font-size: 13px; color: rgba(26, 26, 46, 0.7);">{{ answer.answer_label }}</div>
  </div>
  <span style="display: inline-block; width: 10px; height: 10px; min-width: 10px; border-radius: 50%; background: {{ answer.band_color }}; flex-shrink: 0; margin-top: 4px;"></span>
  <span style="font-size: 14px; font-weight: 600; color: #008ecf; min-width: 48px; text-align: right; white-space: nowrap; flex-shrink: 0;">{{ "%.2f"|format(answer.score) }}</span>
</div>
```
Keep `align-items: flex-start` on `.answers-row` (question text can wrap to 2+ lines) but add `margin-top: 4px` on the dot span so it roughly optically centers against the first line of text rather than pinning to the very top — this is a minor visual nicety, not required for the clipping fix itself; if it looks off in the human-verify pass, `align-items: center` combined with `align-self: flex-start` on the text column is an acceptable alternative (call this out to the human reviewer in Task 2, do not silently guess and skip verification).

**Fix #5b (wording):** in the `.expert-help` box, change the first sentence from "The Centre of Excellence is here to help you navigate your dataspace maturity journey." to "The Data Spaces Support Centre is here to help you navigate your dataspace maturity journey." No other text in that paragraph changes.

Do not touch `.priority-row`, `.legend`, `.radar-wrap`, or any other already-fixed block in this file — those are out of scope and already correct per Phase 16-05.
